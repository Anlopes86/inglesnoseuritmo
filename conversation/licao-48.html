Warm-up: Yes, No, or Maybe?

Answer quickly: yes, no, or maybe — and give one reason.

Will people still carry smartphones in 2050?

Will cash, keys, and passwords disappear?

Will most homes have a robot that does at least one chore?

Would you eat a meal made by a 3D food printer?

Key Expressions

A thing of the past

Something that is no longer used or common.
"Physical wallets may be a thing of the past by 2050."

To become the norm

To become usual, expected, or standard.
"Working with AI assistants could become the norm."

Just around the corner

Likely to happen very soon.
"Fully driverless taxis may be just around the corner."

To take over

To assume control or replace someone in a task.
"Robots may take over the most dangerous jobs."

To rely on

To depend on someone or something.
"We already rely on apps to organize our day."

To sound far-fetched

To seem strange, extreme, or difficult to believe.
"A hotel on the Moon sounds far-fetched — for now."
